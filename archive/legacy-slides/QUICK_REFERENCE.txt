KEY TECHNICAL PRINCIPLES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TRANSPARENCY:
  • In PptxGenJS: transparency: 85 means 85% transparent (15% opaque)
  • Glass card = high transparency (12% = very transparent)
  • Background ellipses = very high transparency (75-92%)
  • Dividers/accents = medium transparency (40-60%)

SHADOWS (Glass Floating Effect):
  • High blur radius (30-40pt) = soft, diffused shadow
  • Small Y-offset (6-8pt) = card appears to float slightly above
  • Very low opacity (0.08-0.10) = subtle, not harsh

ROUNDED CORNERS:
  • rectRadius: 0.2 inches = 24pt (primary cards)
  • rectRadius: 0.167 inches = 20pt (secondary cards)
  • PptxGenJS expects inches; convert: pt ÷ 96 = inches

COLOR BLENDING:
  • No native gradients in PPTX
  • Solution: Overlapping transparent shapes
  • Result: Smooth color transition

Z-ORDER (Layer Depth):
  • Add shapes in this order: Background → Containers → Text
  • PptxGenJS renders in addition order (first added = bottom layer)
  • Text always added last (top layer)
