## 2. Purpose

The Community Anchor Organization ("Anchor") agrees to pilot WeaveNYC as a community node in its service area, helping local residents discover official food and social-service resources and safely match neighbor-to-neighbor offers of help. The WeaveNYC Project Team ("WeaveNYC") agrees to provide the software, training, and on-call support required for the pilot.

This MOU is a non-binding expression of intent between the Anchor and the WeaveNYC project. It does not create a joint venture, employment relationship, or financial obligation between the parties.

---

## 3. Anchor Responsibilities

During the pilot term, the Anchor agrees to:

1. **Moderation.** Review posts that land in the `pending_review` queue within 24 hours of being flagged, during normal business days. Reasonable delays for weekends, holidays, and illness are expected and acceptable.
2. **Content review.** Make moderation decisions (approve / takedown / escalate) using the rules agreed with the WeaveNYC team at onboarding. Apply rules consistently across residents.
3. **Announcements.** Publish at least one community announcement per month (e.g., pantry hours, closures, drives) so residents see an active anchor presence.
4. **Report handling.** Triage user-submitted reports (harassment, fraud, safety) and act on them within 24 hours of flagging.
5. **Check-ins.** Attend a weekly 30-minute check-in call during the first 30 days of the pilot, and a monthly 30-minute check-in thereafter.
6. **Confidentiality of credentials.** Store anchor credentials, TOTP secrets, and recovery codes securely. Do not share credentials with anyone outside the Anchor organization. Notify WeaveNYC within 24 hours of any suspected credential compromise.
7. **No unauthorized access.** Use the anchor admin dashboard only for the purposes described in this MOU. Do not attempt to access the underlying database, servers, or infrastructure.
8. **No public spokesperson role.** Do not speak to press or media on behalf of WeaveNYC. Direct all media inquiries to the WeaveNYC Project Team. Anchors may of course speak publicly about their own organization's work.

---

## 4. WeaveNYC Team Responsibilities

During the pilot term, WeaveNYC agrees to:

1. **Technical support.** Maintain the WeaveNYC platform in working order, including the anchor admin dashboard, resource directory, and resident-facing app.
2. **Incident response.** Provide on-call coverage with a target acknowledgment of 15 minutes for P0 incidents (service outage, data leak, safety emergency) on a 24/7 basis, and 2 business hours for P1 incidents.
3. **Training.** Provide a one-hour training session before the Anchor goes live, plus written documentation in the Anchor's preferred language (English, Spanish, or Simplified Chinese).
4. **Credentialing.** Provision the Anchor account, issue the invite code through a confidential channel, and assist with TOTP activation and recovery-code storage.
5. **Analytics.** Share a monthly impact summary (aggregate resident matches, post volume, moderation workload, response times) specific to the Anchor's community.
6. **Feature requests.** Maintain a public issue tracker and review Anchor-submitted feature requests at a weekly triage meeting. WeaveNYC does not commit to implementing any specific request.
7. **Data-handling transparency.** Notify the Anchor within 72 hours of any discovered data incident affecting residents in the Anchor's community.
8. **Attribution.** Display the Anchor organization's name and logo (with permission) on the community page, the project website, and annual impact reports.

---

## 5. Data Handling & Privacy

1. **What the Anchor sees.** The Anchor's admin account can view posts, reports, moderation history, and aggregate community analytics for the Anchor's own community only. The Anchor cannot see other communities' data. The Anchor cannot see residents' real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise GPS coordinates — only community-scoped content and hashed actor identifiers.
2. **What WeaveNYC stores.** WeaveNYC stores only what is necessary to run the service: pseudonymous user records, posts, moderation actions, and analytics events. WeaveNYC does not store government-issued IDs, immigration status, credit information, or health records.
3. **No sale of data.** WeaveNYC will not sell, rent, or trade resident data. WeaveNYC will not share identifiable resident data with any third party except as required by valid legal process.
4. **Privacy obligations.** The Anchor agrees to treat any resident information it encounters as confidential, to not copy it outside the WeaveNYC platform, and to not use it for purposes outside this pilot.
5. **Retention at project end.** On project shutdown or Anchor termination (Section 7): within 30 days, WeaveNYC will either (a) hand off anonymized aggregate analytics to the Anchor for its records, or (b) delete the community's records entirely, at the Anchor's written election. Soft-deleted resident accounts follow the existing 30-day purge schedule enforced by the platform.
6. **Legal process.** If WeaveNYC receives a subpoena, warrant, or other compulsory legal demand for resident data, WeaveNYC will, to the extent legally permitted, notify the affected Anchor and resident before disclosure.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this MOU for any reason on 14 days' written notice to the other. Either party may terminate immediately, without prior notice, for:

- A material safety incident (imminent threat to a resident, anchor, or staff member)
- A suspected data breach affecting community residents
- A material breach of Section 3 or Section 4 that is not cured within 7 days of written notice
- Bad-faith use of the platform (fraud, discriminatory moderation, public misrepresentation of WeaveNYC)

On termination, WeaveNYC will deprovision the Anchor's admin credentials within 24 hours and apply the data-retention choice described in Section 5.5.

## 8. Other Terms

1. **No fees.** No money changes hands in either direction during the pilot.
2. **Intellectual property.** WeaveNYC retains all rights to the WeaveNYC software. Anchor retains all rights to its own name, logo, and content.
3. **Liability.** This is a student-led pilot. Neither party assumes legal liability for the acts or omissions of residents using the platform. The WeaveNYC platform is provided "as is" during the pilot term.
4. **Governing understanding.** This MOU reflects a good-faith collaboration. Disputes will be resolved in good-faith conversation between the named primary contacts before any escalation.
5. **Amendments.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
